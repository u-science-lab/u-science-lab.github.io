01 Image analysis

U-FISH

Find the signal.
Keep the detail.

Detect fluorescent spots across diverse FISH images. A compact neural network enhances the image before locating spots, with support for both 2D images and 3D volumes.

  • 2D & 3D detection
  • Python & CLI
  • napari plugin

02 Probe design

U-Probe

Your experiment.
Your probe architecture.

Describe probe components and constraints in YAML, then assemble and filter candidates in a reproducible workflow. Use established FISH designs or create custom structures, with optional AI-agent assistance.

  • Custom architectures
  • Automated quality checks
  • Agent-ready
